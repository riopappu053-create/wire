FAQs

Good questions, straight answers.

Still unsure about something? Give us a call — we're happy to talk it through before you book.

Call Us Ask a Question
Can you fit a fixture I bought myself?
Absolutely — most of our customers supply their own fittings. Just share a photo or model number beforehand so we can confirm the ceiling support and wiring are suitable.
Do I need a new ceiling box for a heavy chandelier?
Usually, yes. Heavy fixtures need a properly rated ceiling box or brace. We assess this on site and install the correct support before hanging anything.
Will my LED lights flicker with a dimmer?
Not with the right dimmer. LED-compatible dimmers are matched to your fitting's load, and we test for flicker and buzzing before we finish.
How long does a typical fitting take?
A single standard light takes around 30–45 minutes. Ceiling fans and chandeliers take longer — typically 1–2 hours depending on the support and wiring required.
Do you tidy up after fitting?
Always. Dust sheets go down before we start, and all packaging, off-cuts and debris are removed when we're done.
