Home Safety Audit

Warning Signs Your Wiring Needs Immediate Attention

Hidden electrical faults are the leading cause of residential structure fires. Never ignore these early warning indicators inside your home.

Frequent Tripping

Circuit breakers that repeatedly trip when running kitchen appliances or HVAC units indicate dangerous circuit overloads or short circuits.

High Fire Risk

Flickering & Dimming

Lights that dim when the AC kicks on or flicker randomly point to loose neutral connections or deteriorating service lugs.

Arcing Hazard

Warm or Scorched Outlets

Wall plates that feel warm to the touch, emit a faint fishy or plastic odor, or show brown discoloration require immediate shutoff.

Critical Emergency

Buzzing Switchboards

Audible humming, crackling, or buzzing sounds inside your main breaker panel mean a breaker is failing to trip under load.

Common Questions

Electrical Service FAQs

Do I need a permit for a panel upgrade?

Yes. All main switchboard upgrades and new sub-panels require a municipal permit and utility coordination. Our team handles 100% of the paperwork and inspector sign-off for you.

Can you rewire my home without tearing down walls?

In most homes, yes. We use flexible fiberglass fish tapes, attic/crawlspace access, and existing conduit paths to pull new copper wiring with minimal to zero drywall cutting.

How fast is your emergency response?

Our on-call master electricians are stationed across the metro area and typically arrive within 60 to 90 minutes for urgent power outages, sparking panels, or storm damage.

What does your 12-month warranty cover?

Every circuit, breaker, switch, or fixture we supply and install is covered for 12 full months. If any connection or component fails, we return and replace it free of charge.
