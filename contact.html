FAQs

Before you get in touch.

A few quick answers to the questions we hear most. Anything else, just ask — we're happy to help.

Call Us Email Us
How quickly will I hear back?
Most enquiries get a call-back within 60 minutes during office hours. Emergency calls are answered straight away, 24 hours a day.
Is the on-site quote really free?
Yes. We attend, assess the job and provide a fixed written quote at no charge and with no obligation to proceed.
Do you charge a call-out fee?
There's no separate call-out fee for standard bookings within our service areas. Any travel charges are agreed in writing first.
Can you come out the same day?
Same-day slots are usually available for metro and north-suburb addresses. Book early in the day for the best chance of a same-day visit.
What payment methods do you accept?
Card, bank transfer and cash are all accepted. For larger projects a deposit secures the booking, with the balance due on completion.
