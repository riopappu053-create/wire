What Affects Your Price

Four factors, fully explained.

We're not hiding anything — these are the only things that can move a quote up or down. Every job is assessed against them.

01

Job Complexity

A straightforward swap costs less than a diagnostic-heavy fault. We quote based on the work required, not the time we're there.

02

Parts & Materials

Quality fittings are included at cost in every written quote. Nothing is marked up, and you see exactly what you're paying for.

03

Access & Location

Easy-access jobs keep labour low. Awkward ceilings, tight roof spaces or long cable runs add time — but we always explain why first.

04

Urgency & Timing

Standard bookings have no extra charge. Same-day, after-hours or weekend callouts may carry a small premium — always agreed first.

Pricing FAQs

Your pricing questions, answered.

Straight answers to the things customers ask us most about cost.

Do you charge for a quote?
Never. On-site inspections and written quotes are completely free within our service areas — no obligation to proceed.
Are parts included in the price?
Yes — standard fittings are included at cost in the written quote. If you'd like premium fixtures, we can supply them or use ones you've already bought.
What if the job takes longer than quoted?
Our quotes are fixed. If we discover something unexpected on site, we stop, explain it clearly and provide a revised quote — nothing is added without your approval.
Do you offer a warranty?
Every job comes with a 12-month workmanship warranty. Parts carry their own manufacturer warranties, which we'll document for you.
Can I get a discount for multiple jobs?
Yes. Bundling multiple jobs into one visit often reduces the overall cost. Mention this when you book and we'll factor it into the quote.
