Safety Audit

Recognize The Telltale Signs of Faulty Wiring.

Outdated or overloaded electrical systems silently compromise your property safety, drive up energy waste, and risk costly equipment damage.

Tripping Breakers

Frequent breaker trips indicate overloaded circuits or short-circuiting wires hidden behind your drywall.

High Fire Hazard

Flickering Lights

Unstable illumination when heavy appliances start points to severe voltage drops and loose service lines.

Circuit Strain

Warm Outlet Plates

Switches or sockets that feel warm or emit a faint burning smell signal dangerous internal arcing.

Immediate Risk

Obsolete Fuse Boxes

Legacy 60-amp or screw-in fuse panels cannot handle modern smart home power requirements safely.

Out of Code
